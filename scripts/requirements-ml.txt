# existing drift on the Modal side, out of scope for this issue).

# combination throws `TypeError: check_array() got an unexpected keyword
# argument 'force_all_finite'` on every predict_proba() call, confirmed live
# this session. lightgbm==4.6.0 dropped that parameter from its shim and was
# verified live against the real ensemble.pkl artifact with no error.

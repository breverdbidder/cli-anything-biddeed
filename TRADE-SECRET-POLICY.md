## What Is Protected

The following categories of intellectual property are trade secrets of Everest Capital USA:

1. **Shapira Formula parameters** — Jurisdiction-specific ARV multipliers, bid thresholds,
   and buffer values tuned per zip code and auction type.
2. **XGBoost model weights** — Trained on proprietary Brevard County foreclosure and
   tax deed auction outcomes spanning 10+ years.
3. **Reward function constants** — Asymmetric penalty/reward weights governing the
   reinforcement learning bid-optimization loop.
4. **Historical training dataset** — Thousands of Brevard County foreclosure + tax deed
   outcomes with actual prices, buyer types, and property characteristics.
5. **CMA adjustment coefficients** — Per-feature dollar adjustments derived from
   20 years of manual CMA expertise.
6. **Jurisdiction opportunity scoring thresholds** — Signal weights identifying
   undervalued markets ("the next Palm Bay" pattern).
7. **Behavioral matching model weights** — PostHog-derived buyer preference
   refinement parameters.
8. **Agent skill library** — Self-improved procedural knowledge accumulated
   through operational experience.

## Legal Basis

| Statute | Scope |
|---|---|
| Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA), 18 U.S.C. § 1836 | Federal — civil + criminal |
| Florida Uniform Trade Secrets Act (FUTSA), Fla. Stat. § 688.001-009 | State — injunctive + damages |

Both statutes require the owner to take "reasonable measures" to maintain secrecy.
This policy document, code-level headers, NDA requirements, and private repository
settings constitute those reasonable measures.

What They Do

Algoma collapses the pre-construction developer workflow — zoning lookup, site capacity, market comps, pro forma, renderings — into a single AI-powered tool. Their pitch: a small developer evaluating an infill site previously needed $5K–$25K in consultant fees and 4–8 weeks. Algoma delivers results in days. Tagline: "Address to investor-ready."

Feature Inventory (8 modules)

  • Site Overview — Parcel fundamentals, lot size, zoning, owner info, site risks
  • Zoning Analysis — Height, setbacks, FAR, density, permitted uses in natural language
  • Market Intelligence — Housing supply comps, permit activity, demographic trends
  • Comp Builder — Filter comparable sales/rentals by proximity, recency, building class
  • Site Capacity / AI Massing — Unit yield, buildable sqft, FAR utilization based on zoning
  • Pro Forma Generator — Construction costs, NOI, cap rate, IRR projections
  • AI Renderings — Architectural visualizations of proposed buildings
  • Feasibility Packages — Expert-assisted packages (service tier)

One-line positioning

"BidDeed.AI: parcel-level, auction-event data for actual foreclosure/tax-deed bidders — a resolved sale, a lien hierarchy, a bid ceiling, for one address.

Reventure: the best free housing-market-trend content on YouTube, backing a ZIP/metro-level forecasting app — built for someone deciding whether to buy a house, not someone bidding at a courthouse auction next week."

When you win / lose

✅ You win when the prospect needs

  • A specific parcel's auction outcome, lien position, or bid ceiling — Reventure has no parcel/auction product
  • An event signal ("this sold yesterday, here's the resolved buyer") — Reventure's unit of analysis stops at ZIP/metro
  • An 18-section per-property report — Reventure has no per-property report of any kind
  • Florida foreclosure/tax-deed specific depth

❌ You lose when the prospect needs

  • A brand they already trust from years of free YouTube content (~681K subscribers vs. our ~0)
  • A polished consumer mobile app (iOS + Android, 4.5–4.7★) — we have none today
  • National ZIP/metro-level price-forecast trend data, not FL auction-day data
  • A low, simple $39/mo consumer price point vs. a comparably-priced enterprise-feeling report

Objection handling

"I already watch [that housing YouTube channel] / use that app."
Great signal — that means you already care about this data. Ask directly: does it tell you what a specific address sold for at auction yesterday, or who the resolved buyer was? It can't — its unit of analysis stops at the ZIP code. We start where that data ends: one parcel, one auction, one bid ceiling.
"They have hundreds of thousands of subscribers, you have none."
True, and it's the single biggest gap on this scorecard — see the steal list for exactly how we're closing it (video-as-product-demo is item #1). We're not claiming their reach yet. We're claiming a narrower, deeper edge on data no ZIP-level forecast app can produce: what actually happened at a specific courthouse auction this week.
"Their app is nicer / they have a mobile app and you don't."
Fair — their consumer polish and app-store presence beat us today, logged here as an open gap, not waved away. What we have that a ZIP-level app structurally cannot build without becoming a different product: parcel-level auction data and a resolved-lien hierarchy for one address.

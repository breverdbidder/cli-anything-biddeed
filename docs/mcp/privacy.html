Privacy Policy

BidDeed.AI MCP Server · Everest Capital USA · Last updated 2026-07-02

This policy covers the BidDeed.AI Model Context Protocol (MCP) server (biddeed-mcp), reachable at https://biddeed.ai/api/mcp and via the biddeed-mcp npm package. It applies to any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or other MCP client) that connects using a BidDeed API key or a WorkOS-issued OAuth token.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, name, tier, and (for OAuth users) your WorkOS user ID — used to authenticate and meter API calls.
  • Tool call metadata: tool name, county/parcel parameters, latency, cache hits, and error class — used for billing (Stripe Billing Meter) and reliability monitoring (PostHog). We do not log full request/response bodies beyond what is needed for billing reconciliation.
  • Auction watch subscriptions: if you call watch_auction, we store the case number, county, and the notification email/phone you provide, solely to deliver the alerts you requested.

What we don't collect

  • We do not use your queries to train models.
  • We do not sell tool-call data to third parties.
  • We do not store your MCP client's conversation history — only the discrete tool arguments passed to us.

Third parties

Underlying public-data sources queried by specific tools include the Federal Reserve (FRED, for get_interest_rate / get_market_data) and REISkip/BatchData (for skip_trace, pass-through, billed separately). Auth is handled by WorkOS AuthKit for OAuth logins; we never see your WorkOS password. Billing is handled by Stripe.

Data retention

Billing and usage-metering records are retained for as long as your account is active plus the period required for financial recordkeeping. Auction watch subscriptions are retained until the watched auction resolves or you request removal.

Your rights

Contact support@biddeed.ai to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data. See Support for response-time expectations.

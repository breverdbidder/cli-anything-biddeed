## PART 1 — the validator, fixed (VERIFIED)

`director_qa.check_title_compliance()` called `hook_writer.validate_title()`, which checked word count (5-9), presence of *an* ellipsis (`…` OR `...`), *a count* of exactly 2 emoji anywhere in the string, and third-person phrasing. It never checked emoji **position**, the exact ellipsis **character**, title **case**, whether the title **leaked the payoff figure**, or whether the assigned **archetype matched the row's own facts**. That is why all 20 live variants carried `qa_pass=true` while visibly violating T1 (`docs/gtm/REEL_SPEC_BOLT32.md`).

### The 5 named checks added

| # | Check | Where | Catches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `check_payoff_leak(title, reel)` | `hook_writer.py`, called from `director_qa.check_payoff_leak()` | Title contains the exact sold price / discount pct / opening bid the 20-28s Payoff beat delivers. Assessed-value figures (a Setup-beat fact) and vague stakes language are still allowed. |
| 2 | `check_emoji_placement(title)` | `hook_writer.py` | Any emoji before the ellipsis, or anything other than exactly 2 vocabulary-agnostic emoji immediately after it. |
| 3 | `check_ellipsis_form(title)` | `hook_writer.py` | Literal `"..."` (three periods) instead of the single `"…"` character. |
| 4 | `check_title_case(title)` | `hook_writer.py` | Sentence-case drift (small connective words stay lowercase; every other word and the first word must be capitalized). |
| 5 | `check_archetype_data_match(archetype, reel, third_party_bidder, plaintiff_confirmed_bank)` | `hook_writer.py`, called from `director_qa.check_archetype_data_match()` | `countdown_presale` on a `phase='postsale'` row; `mystery_nobody_bid` where the sale record shows a confirmed third-party bidder; `bank_vs_house` where no bank/lender plaintiff is confirmed in the data. |

Checks 2-4 are also folded into `validate_title()` itself (so future generation self-enforces them, not just QA). All 5 are additionally recorded as **separate named entries in `qa_scores`** with an `observed` value, per the issue's "per-check result" requirement — `title_compliance` alone no longer carries the whole story.

`director_qa.review_reel()` now fetches real `reel_facts` per reel (`fetch_reel_facts()`): the parent `biddeed_reels` row (phase/sale_type/sold_amount/delta_pct/opening_bid/judgment_amount) plus a live `public.multi_county_auctions` lookup for `sale_result`/`winning_bidder`/`plaintiff`, computing `third_party_bidder` and `plaintiff_confirmed_bank`. A genuine miss on that lookup leaves both `None` (treated as "not confirmed"), never fabricated — same pattern as `banned_names_for_case()` elsewhere in this pipeline.

## PART 2 — regenerate + render

### 2a. Title + archetype regeneration (VERIFIED — applied live to all 20 rows)

**Archetype/data-match audit** (`director_qa.fetch_reel_facts()` against live `multi_county_auctions`): all 5 properties are `phase='postsale'`, `sale_result='SOLD_THIRD_PARTY'` with a named `winning_bidder`, and `plaintiff` is `NULL` on all 5 (a real, confirmed data gap — not something this issue's scope authorizes backfilling). Consequence: for every one of the 5 reels, the only archetypes `check_archetype_data_match()` accepts are `{shock_number, underdog_bidder, red_flag_warning, hidden_value_reveal}` — `countdown_presale` (phase mismatch), `mystery_nobody_bid` (a bidder is confirmed), and `bank_vs_house` (no confirmed bank plaintiff) are invalid for all 20 rows today. 8 of the 20 variants had one of those 3 invalid archetypes and were reassigned to the smallest set of not-yet-used valid archetypes within their own reel (never breaking the `unique(reel_id, archetype)` constraint).

**Title regeneration**: deterministic per-archetype templates (`agents/reel_studio/_cp3c_b_regen.py`), not a live Hook Writer LLM call. **INFERRED / logged deviation**: chosen over re-invoking `router_client`/OpenRouter because it is free, deterministic, and every candidate is validated against all 5 named checks (plus word count/third-person) *before* being written to the row — zero risk of an LLM hallucinating a 6th payoff-leaking title. The mechanism `generate_variants_for_reel()` itself uses for *new* reels is unchanged; this was a one-time repair pass over the 20 already-shipped rows.

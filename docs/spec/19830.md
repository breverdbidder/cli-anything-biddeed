I re-fetched all of §45.031 (`http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0000-0099/0045/Sections/0045.031.html`) specifically to check this and found no such fixed period in the statute text — it says only "within the prescribed period" with the period left to the court's order. Per the Honesty Protocol ("BLANK > WRONG"), I did not add the judge's unverified claim to the content. **This surfaced a real, valuable correction in round 1** (see below) but round 2's specific claim is not incorporated.

**Genuine improvement from the process (not a dissent):** round 1 of `deposits-and-payment` originally cited only §45.031 (judicial-sale/foreclosure procedure) for a question scoped to "foreclosure or tax deed." A live re-fetch of §197.542 (tax deed sale procedure) confirmed tax deed sales run under a **different** statute with a **different** deadline: a nonrefundable 5%-or-$200 deposit and a 24-hour full-payment window, vs. foreclosure's court-set period under §45.031. The published file now cites both statutes and states the distinction. This is exactly the kind of catch the A9 process exists to make, and it worked.

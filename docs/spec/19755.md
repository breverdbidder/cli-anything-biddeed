## Design decisions worth flagging

1. **Sign convention (critical, previously undocumented in practice).**
   `finance.bank_transactions.amount_cents`: positive = outflow, negative = inflow (per the
   table's original doc comment — confirmed against real SimpleFIN raw payloads). The
   *separate* `finance.recon_run()` migration (`20260902k`) documents the **opposite**
   convention for its own R1/R2 matching logic and explicitly warns "whoever wires real
   ingestion in MUST negate Plaid's raw sign... or R1/R2 will silently match everything
   backwards." This migration's functions use the *table's* convention (verified correct
   against raw payloads), independent of `recon_run()`'s internal assumption — `recon_run()`
   was not modified.
2. **Universal transfer sign rule.** For any transfer pair, the leg with `amount_cents<0`
   (inflow) is DEBITED and the leg with `amount_cents>0` (outflow) is CREDITED, regardless
   of account TYPE (asset checking vs. liability credit card) — a payment reducing a
   liability is, mechanically, also a debit. This let one code path handle intra-entity,
   inter-entity, and card-payment-from-a-different-entity cases without special-casing.
3. **Self-mask bug found and fixed in-session.** `ONLINE PAYMENT THANK YOU` (Visa 2308 side)
   has no mask in its own text, so it's hardcoded to mask `2308` — which is the
   transaction's *own* account, not the counterpart's. An earlier version of
   `post_bank_transfer()` bailed out on this self-mask case, producing 6 false
   `transfer_no_counterpart` exceptions (silently overwritten with a real match once the
   opposite/checking-side leg ran its own search and found this row directly — a genuine
   idempotency gap, not just cosmetic). Fixed by broadening the self-mask branch to search
   all *other* linked accounts; verified: re-running `process_bank_transactions()` is now a
   true no-op except for the 1 genuine gap.
4. **4100 vs 4000 deviation (per CC_META_PROMPT §2.3 — "the DoD query itself may be
   wrong").** The issue says "Unmatched → 5900 Other Expense / 4100 Other Income." Neither
   `ariel_personal` nor `everest_capital_brevard` has a `4100` account — on the other 5
   entities `4100` specifically means "Revenue – Fact Finder" (FF billing), irrelevant here.
   Both entities' generic revenue bucket is `4000` (verified live), used instead. Logged
   here rather than silently substituted and reported green.
5. **Recurring-cost run-rate guard.** 3 large one-off distribution wires ("WT 26...", Bank
   Hapoalim/Yahav) landed 8–10 days apart and were initially classified `weekly` cadence →
   a **$125,310.79/mo phantom run-rate** that would have badly corrupted burn/runway. Added
   `category_rules.is_recurring_eligible` (false for `WT 26`/`WT FED#`) to exclude
   one-off-by-nature patterns from `v_recurring_costs` even if they happen to recur by
   coincidence.

## Findings (not the task, but worth Ariel's attention)

1. **The commingling finding cuts both ways.** The issue asked specifically about business
   infra paid from the *personal* account (built as `v_commingled_business_costs`). But
   `everest_capital_brevard`'s own business checking is *also* paying for clearly
   cross-business SaaS/vendor costs that don't belong to the Brevard property: Anthropic (5
   ACH payments), BidDeed.AI (2 ACH payments — Brevard paying its own sister company),
   Tracerfy/Link.com (BidDeed.AI's skip-trace vendor), ElevenLabs, and Rocket Money (a
   *personal*-finance subscription app charged to the business account — the reverse
   direction again). This is a second, real commingling pattern the issue didn't scope a
   view for; flagged in `category_rules.note` per row but not built into a second schema
   object (avoiding scope creep — Ariel should decide whether this needs its own view).
2. **Large wire transfers need Ariel's own review, not just a categorization guess.** Two
   title-company wires in April totaling **$108,670.53** (`Spira Law Group P.A. "Seller
   proceeds"` $70,715.09; `Supreme Title Closings, LLC` $37,955.44) landed as revenue
   (`WT FED#` → 4000), and three outbound wires totaling **$86,820.41** went to named
   individuals at Bank Hapoalim/Bank Yahav (Israel) — categorized `5900 Other Expense` as an
   INFERRED best guess (investor/family distribution or loan repayment tied to a property
   sale). Both entity-side legs post as **drafts** (litigation-gated), so nothing here is
   final without Ariel's review, but the categorization itself is a guess worth confirming.
3. **1 genuine unreconcilable gap**: `ariel_personal` bank txn `b7db6d1c...`, `ONLINE
   PAYMENT THANK YOU`, -$140.00, posted 2026-03-27. No transaction anywhere in the 616-row
   dataset matches this amount within ±3 days on any of the 4 linked accounts — the
   counterpart leg (presumably a checking-side "TO WELLS FARGO VISA...2308" transfer) was
   never synced. Left as an open `recon_exceptions` row (`reason='transfer_no_counterpart'`)
   rather than guessed.
4. **The 6160 WF Business Market Rate Savings account should be added to SimpleFIN** — per
   the issue's own note. It funds/receives real cash movements (11 sweep transactions,
   $150/mo recurring + several ad-hoc transfers) that currently only exist as one-sided
   entries against the new unlinked `1002` ledger account.

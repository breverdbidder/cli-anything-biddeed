## Regression Caught Mid-Session (G: 100% → 0% → 100%)

The first fix attempt inserted 7 `parcel_zones` rows (zone codes R-3, RSF-2, PUD×4, WATER) under jurisdiction 865 but only R-3 already had a matching `zoning_districts`/`zone_standards` row. RSF-2/PUD/WATER are Unincorporated Nassau County codes that had never been ingested anywhere in the system. Result: `v_zoning_district_applicability` had no record for those 3 codes, so the KPI view treated them as measurable-but-missing rather than N/A — G's density coverage dropped from 100% to 82.4% and far/pk1000 became spuriously measurable at 0%, tanking the overall G metric to 0.0.

This was **not caught by the workflow's adversarial refuters** (they covered C/D/I/cross-county-regression but not G) — it was caught by a manual full-scoreboard re-check after the workflow reported success. Fix:
1. Deleted the 6 non-R-3 `parcel_zones` rows (kept R-3, which was already correct).
2. Backfilled real `zoning_districts` + `zone_standards` for RSF-2/PUD/WATER under jurisdiction 865, sourced from:
   - Nassau LDC Art. 9 (RS-1/RS-2) via zoneomics.com's Municode republication (Municode itself returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch) — real min lot size (8,700 sqft), setbacks, height.
   - Nassau LDC Art. 25 (PUD) — ordinance sets **no fixed density**; density is controlled by the underlying Future Land Use map (Sec. 25.02(A)).
   - Nassau County's own 2030 Comprehensive Plan Future Land Use Element PDF (nassaucountyfl.com/DocumentCenter/View/29948, effective 07/17/25), Table "Residential Density Standards": LDR 0–2.0 du/a, MDR 0–3.0 du/a, HDR 3–10 du/a.
3. Re-inserted the 6 `parcel_zones` rows.

**Honesty note:** PUD's `max_density_du_acre=3.0` is a district-level approximation (3 of the 4 backfilled PUD parcels are FLU=Medium Density; 1, 95521 Hanover Ct, is FLU=Low Density at 0–2.0 du/a) — labeled INFERRED, `confidence_score=0.5`. WATER's `max_density_du_acre=0.0` reflects the real FutureLandUse=WATER designation (non-buildable), `confidence_score=0.9`. Both documented in `zone_standards.source_url`. No values were guessed without ordinance/comp-plan backing.

Battle Card
Positioning
Full Algoma SWOT/PRD/positioning lives in docs/plans/ALGOMA-CI-REPORT.md (unchanged by this session except for the new §2A tech-stack addition). This card summarizes the three-way read.

Algoma — the workflow tool

Wins when the prospect is a new-construction developer who wants address-to-investor-pitch in one tool: pro forma, AI massing, renderings. Loses on data depth, FL specificity, and any foreclosure/auction use case — it has zero coverage there.

ZoneWise — the data layer

Wins on raw, queryable, DB-verified FL zoning depth + the only one of the three with foreclosure/auction intelligence baked in. Loses on national breadth (Acres) and developer workflow polish (Algoma) — by design, not by gap.

Acres — the nationwide breadth play

Wins on scale: 150M+ parcels, nationwide, an actual AI search agent shipped (not vaporware — "Acres Intelligence," May 2026). Loses on FL-specific auction/tax-deed intelligence entirely, and its own stack shows a company mid-migration (legacy Django monolith still live alongside a newer Next.js product).

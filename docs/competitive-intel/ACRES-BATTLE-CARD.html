When you win / lose

✅ You win when the prospect needs

  • FL foreclosure/tax-deed auction calendars — Acres has zero
  • ARV-based BID/REVIEW/SKIP deal scoring — Acres has zero
  • Raw, queryable FL zone codes/standards/uses, not AI summaries
  • A founder-operator's FL-specific domain judgment
  • A lower-cost tool scoped to FL, not a nationwide enterprise platform

❌ You lose when the prospect needs

  • Nationwide coverage (150M+ parcels, 30,000+ jurisdictions)
  • A natural-language AI research agent (Acres Intelligence)
  • Enterprise portfolio collaboration (tagging, comments, shared maps)
  • A mobile app (Acres has iOS + Android live)
  • Land valuation / comp reports for non-foreclosure acquisitions

Objection handling

"We're already evaluating Acres.com."
Acres is a strong nationwide choice — one of the best-funded, best-reviewed platforms in that category. Ask directly: do they have a FL tax-deed/foreclosure auction calendar or ARV-based bid math? They don't — it's not their product.
"Acres has an AI agent, you don't."
True, and a real gap we're tracking. Acres Intelligence answers acquisition/feasibility questions — it doesn't compute a BID/REVIEW/SKIP recommendation against a real courthouse auction calendar. Different job.
"Acres covers the whole country, you're FL-only."
True, and if you need 50-state coverage, Acres wins outright. For FL specifically — the most active foreclosure/tax-deed market in the US — we go deeper on a use case Acres doesn't build for at all.
"Acres is backed by $60M+ and has CBRE as a customer."
Real, and it's AcreTrader's balance sheet behind Acres, not a bootstrap operation. We're not claiming to out-fund them — we're claiming a narrower, sharper edge: 10+ years of FL foreclosure investing built directly into the product, zero invented data.

## What it is

Winner Data is the **Florida property-data / intelligence PLATFORM LAYER** for
the Everest Capital USA ecosystem, built on a **10.5M-parcel moat**. It
supplies **all leads** for `biddeed.ai`, `zonewise.ai`, and Protection
Partners — those products are consumers of Winner Data's resolved signals,
not separate lead-generation efforts.

## What it is NOT

Winner Data is **not an auction-lead vendor**, **not a mover-lead vendor**,
**not a distressed-property engine**, and **not a foreclosure business**.
Those are downstream verticals it happens to feed today, not what the
company is. Describing Winner Data by any single vertical it currently
serves is the exact drift this canon exists to stop.

## Buyer avatar

Businesses buying resolved property signals: insurance agencies, moving
companies, contractors, investors and small developers. Specifically the
self-serve small-B2B and B2C segment that ATTOM and Cotality will not onboard
(too small a check size for their enterprise sales motion).

## Subject avatar

Two populations, **never contacted directly by Winner Data**:
- MLS active + pending sellers (non-distressed — this is the current build)
- Auction winners

## Moat

**One resolution event, N verticals.** Once a property signal is resolved
once, adding a new vertical is a template on top of existing resolved data —
not a new data-acquisition pipeline. This is what makes the platform-layer
framing literal rather than aspirational: the cost structure of adding a
vertical is templating, not sourcing.

## Proof points

- SIGNAL$ Property Report (18 sections)
- Insurance Fact Finder (Protection Partners) — live
- The same resolved lead resold to movers at zero incremental acquisition cost

## Positioning frame

Category: Property Data & Analytics. Frame: **Lemonade for property
intelligence** — ATTOM sells the analyst (raw data, you do the work);
Winner Data sells the answer (resolved signal, ready to act on).

09 / 11
🩸 HONEST CRITIQUE

What we have to fix — no spin

🚨
#1 Duplication is a silent integrity failure

8× dupes on individual parcels in primary_scrape. Every "deals today" headline we publish is potentially 2× inflated. If a customer cross-checks against the clerk site they'll catch us. Trust killer.

🪪
#2 PO-backfill rows orphaned from identifiers

When we imported 43 PO rows, we didn't join them to our primary records — they sit in multi_county_auctions with NULL case_number AND NULL parcel_id. We can't link these to BCPAO, can't pull title history, can't enrich.

🔭
#3 No ground truth — we don't actually know if 86 is right

We claim broader coverage than PO but we haven't scraped brevardclerk.us to confirm. 86 might still be incomplete. Honest version: "we believe 86 is closer to truth based on multi-source ingestion, but unverified vs the clerk's published list."

📊
#4 Per-deal field depth lags PO

PO has 100% beds/baths/geo. We don't. Customers using our app for rental analysis or driveby planning will hit "—" frequently. We need BCPAO field API + Mapbox geocoder to match.

## LinkedIn (Ariel Shapira, personal profile — founder voice, first person)

Twenty years buying Florida foreclosures and tax deeds — courthouse steps, then online — taught me the bid was never the hard part. It was everything after: a budget in one spreadsheet, receipts in an email thread, draw requests re-typed for the bank, and a schedule nobody but me was tracking.

I built BidDeed.AI to fix the bid. Today we're shipping the part that comes after it.

A BidDeed Project now carries a deal from the auction calendar to the exit — budget and scopes of work, a schedule, a ledger with receipts and budget-vs-actual, lender draws, a funding pack built from your SIGNAL$ Property Report, and an exit pack when it's time to sell or rent. One project. Not three tools you re-enter the same deal into.

It's live on Pro Plus — three seats included — for anyone who's won a deal and then lost a week rebuilding it somewhere else.

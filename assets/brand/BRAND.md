# Winner Data AI — Brand Block

**Company name:** Winner Data — spoken and written. Never write "WinnerDataAI" in prose; the "AI" belongs in the wordmark and the URL only.

**Domain:** winnerdataai.com (owned, Cloudflare Registrar, Aug 23 2026)

**Tagline (full):** Every Deal Creates a Customer. Our Winner Data AI Finds Them First.

**Short form:** Winner Data finds them first.

## Positioning

Winner Data sells the same auction-winner / new-owner signal across the full real estate chain: insurance producers, realtors, mortgage brokers, attorneys, contractors, and vendors. One dataset, many verticals.

## Usage rules

- One terracotta accent per composition. No second accent colors.
- Minimum clear space around the logo = half the tile height.
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor the tile.
- Never place the terracotta tile on a terracotta background.

Scenario Modeling (What-If Zones)
Side-by-side zone comparisons for any parcel. "What if this were zoned C-2 instead of R-1?" — unit count delta, FAR delta, pro forma delta. Critical for upzoning investment thesis.

PHASE 17 M · 2 weeks
Construction Cost Estimator (FL)
Per-unit + per-SF construction cost by typology (wood-frame / podium / high-rise) and FL region. Updated quarterly from RS Means. Hard cost range + soft cost estimate in pro forma.
TestFit High impact
PHASE 07 L · 4 weeks
Pre-Construction Pro Forma Generator
Full pro forma from parcel + unit program: total dev cost, NOI, cap rate, IRR at 5-year hold. FL regional construction costs + market comps. One-click PDF. Algoma parity.
Algoma Very High impact
PHASE 04 M · 2 weeks
Mixed-Use / Split-Zone Parcel Analysis
Detect parcels spanning multiple zoning districts. Show % in each zone. Flag development implications — which zone governs per stricter-zone rule. Data accuracy improvement.
Gridics Medium impact
PHASE 08 M · 2 weeks
Entitlement Timeline Estimator
Per-jurisdiction entitlement timeline estimates: by-right (0–30 days), special exception (3–6 months), rezoning (6–18 months). Based on FL municipal code analysis.
Algoma Medium impact
PHASE 09 M · 2 weeks
Entitlement Risk Score
LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk score per development plan. Factors: jurisdiction approval history, comp plan alignment, environmental overlays, neighborhood opposition risk indicators.
Algoma Medium impact
PHASE 11 M · 2 weeks
Parking Requirements Calculator
FL per-zone parking minimums and maximums for residential + commercial uses. Accounts for downtown exceptions, TDM allowances, bicycle substitutions. Output: required spaces + land area needed.
Gridics TestFit Medium impact
PHASE 12 M · 2 weeks
Unit Mix Optimizer
Given target unit count + FL zip market data, recommend optimal studio/1BR/2BR/3BR split to maximize NOI. Blended rent per SF + projected annual income.
TestFit Medium impact
PHASE 20 L · 3–4 weeks
Developer API v2 (REST + Webhooks)
Production-grade REST API with JWT auth, rate limiting, versioning, webhooks. Endpoints: parcel lookup, zone standards, unit count, pro forma. Webhooks: zone code change alerts. B2B revenue channel.
Gridics Very High impact
PHASE 05 XL · 6–8 wks/state
National Coverage (GA + TX Phase 1)
Expand beyond FL. First markets: Georgia (Fulton/DeKalb/Gwinnett) and Texas (Harris/Dallas/Travis). FL GIO pipeline pattern adapted for state-specific cadastral sources.
Gridics Very High impact
PHASE 13 L · 3–4 weeks
Building Program Analyzer
For a site + development program: calculate ground floor footprint, floor count, gross SF, NRA, parking levels, amenity SF. Supports garden-style, wrap, podium, stacked flat typologies.
TestFit Medium impact
PHASE 16 L · 3–4 weeks
Variance & Exception Tracker
Track active variance requests + special exceptions filed against FL parcels. Surfaces "hidden upzoning" — parcels where owners are seeking relief. Sourced from FL county planning portals.

Net Rentable Area (NRA) Calculator
Net rentable area from gross building SF. Accounts for core/shell factor, corridors, mechanical spaces, parking. Per-typology efficiency ratios from FL market data. Within 5% of actual delivered projects.
TestFit Low–Med impact
